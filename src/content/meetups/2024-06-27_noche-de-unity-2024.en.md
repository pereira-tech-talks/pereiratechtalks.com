## Unity Night: first steps, shaders & build profiles

On June 27, 2024 we gathered at **Camellando H+W** for **Unity Night: first steps, shaders & build profiles**.

**Talks:**

**Talk 1:**

**Speaker:** Ricardo Barrios

**Role:** Software Engineer, Features at Unity Technologies

**Introduction to shaders in Unity**

An introduction to the concepts you need when you start working with shaders in
Unity: how a shader is structured, and what its functions do when the game is
rendered.

**Talk 2:**

**Speaker:** Alex Cardona

**Role:** Senior Software Engineer, Platforms at Unity Technologies

**Build Profiles in Unity 6**

A look at Build Profiles, a new way to configure how your project builds for
every platform you need in Unity 6.
