## UTP internal programming marathon

On Saturday June 2 the Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira held the **Maratón
Interna UTP 2018**, organized by the In-silico research group. The competition
drew 11 teams from the Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira (UTP) and the
Universidad Católica de Pereira (UCP), each with 3 members, facing a range of
programming challenges.
