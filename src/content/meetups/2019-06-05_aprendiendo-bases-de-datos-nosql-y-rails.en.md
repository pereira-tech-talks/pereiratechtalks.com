## JUNE 5: Learning about NoSQL databases and Ruby on Rails

📣 An exciting June ahead. Two excellent talks on non-relational databases and the
Ruby on Rails framework:

🔎 NoSQL databases have evolved to the point of being indispensable for many
projects and analyses. But what are these databases? How do they differ from the
traditional model? And which databases belong to this paradigm? This talk answers
those questions and gives an overview of a paradigm that has been changing how we
think about storing information.

### Talks

1. **Introduction to NoSQL databases** — Germán Grandas ([profile](https://twitter.com/germangrandas))
2. **Build possibilities with Rails** — Jonatan Gutiérrez ([profile](https://twitter.com/jotangutierrez))
