## Experiences from the software industry night

Speaker: Andrés Rodriguez

Talk: Become a web developer — here are the benefits.

This talk is aimed at people curious about the software industry: students in
technology-adjacent degrees who are unsure which route is the better one to take.
It is also for people already working as developers who want to compare where they
are against what the market offers. We cover salaries, perks, learning paths,
technology trends, and answer questions along the way.
