## Technology with Purpose: Human Connection and Intelligent Collaboration

On November 28, 2024 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Technology with purpose: human connections and intelligent collaboration**.

Join us this November 28 for two excellent talks on using technology with purpose
and collaborating intelligently.

**TALK 1**

**Speaker:** Ana Lopez

**Role:** Electronic engineer with a master's in IT and Telecommunications
Management, and specializations in Business Intelligence and Finance

**Beyond the LLM: the power of multi-agent systems**

How multi-agent systems get past the limits of a single LLM, putting
intelligences to work together to solve complex problems more efficiently and at
greater scale.

**TALK 2**

**Speaker 1:** Catalina Uribe Jaramillo

**Role 1:** UX/UI designer, driven by digital experiences that are conscious and
accessible, built around what people actually need.

**Speaker 2:** María Lizeth

**Role 2:** UX/UI designer focused on driving exponential growth by working with
tech startups and purpose-led companies.

**Software from the human side**

A talk on building technology that is conscious of people — accessible,
functional, empathetic, ethical, inclusive, and diverse. Drawing on their own
experience, Liz and Cata share how design and development teams can build digital
products centered on people's needs, on ethics, and on care for the world around
us.
