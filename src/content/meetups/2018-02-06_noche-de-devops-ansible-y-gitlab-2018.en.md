## DevOps Night: intro to automation with Ansible && CI with GitLab and Docker

We are glad to invite you to our first meetup of the year — an evening dedicated
to DevOps operations.

The number of servers sysadmins have to manage keeps growing fast, pushing them
to find new ways to deploy, configure, and update machines so that mechanical,
repetitive work takes as little time as possible.

### Talks

1. **Introduction to automation with Ansible** — Manuel Pineda ([profile](https://twitter.com/pin3da_))
2. **Learning continuous integration (CI) with GitLab and Docker** — Carlos Gónzales ([profile](http://caal-15.github.io/))
