## The future of AI: code, hardware, and agents

On December 10, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**The future of AI: code, hardware, and agents**. On the programme: Sergio
Alexander Flórez, Sebastián Mora.

Our year-end meetup on how AI is reshaping software engineering — agents, LLMs,
RAG, and AI talking to real hardware.

### Talks

**The future of AI — code, hardware, and agents**

**Speaker:** Sergio Alexander Flórez, Sebastián Mora

A year-closing panel exploring how AI is transforming the way we build software: from writing code to designing agents, orchestrating LLMs, connecting RAGs, and talking to hardware in the real world. What an AI Engineer really is, which tools are shaping the current state of the art (models, agents, MCPs, dev environments), and how programmers can leverage them to ship products and start companies.
