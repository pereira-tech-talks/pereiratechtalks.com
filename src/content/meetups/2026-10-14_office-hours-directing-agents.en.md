## Office Hours: The art of directing agents

On Wednesday 14 October 2026 we met online, at 6:30 p.m. Colombia time, for an Office Hours session: a closer, practical, interactive room with Sergio Alexander Flórez, CTO at DailyBot.

The question of the night: how can one person coordinate several AI agents in parallel and turn hours of supervision into autonomous, verifiable results?

### Talks

**The art of directing agents**

**Speaker:** Sergio Alexander Flórez

How can one person coordinate several AI agents working in parallel and turn hours of supervision into autonomous, verifiable results? Sergio walks through Herdr, a tool for managing agents and workspaces, and Deep Work Plan (DWP), a method for organising, delegating and supervising that work without losing control — with examples, live demos and conversation with the room.
