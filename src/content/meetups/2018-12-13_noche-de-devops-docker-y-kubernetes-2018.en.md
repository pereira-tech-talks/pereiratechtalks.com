## DevOps Night — introduction to Docker and Kubernetes

📣 Announcing our closing meetup. Join us to see 2018 out with the community and
learn plenty about infrastructure and microservice-oriented architectures.

🔎 Docker is a tool built to help developers, testers, and system administrators
alike — with machines, with the environments software runs in, with deployment
processes — letting you build microservice-oriented architectures by boxing each
unit of functionality into containers.

### Talks

1. **Introduction to Docker** — Sergio Florez
2. **Introduction to Kubernetes** — Héctor F. Jiménez ([profile](https://twitter.com/c1b3rh4ck))
