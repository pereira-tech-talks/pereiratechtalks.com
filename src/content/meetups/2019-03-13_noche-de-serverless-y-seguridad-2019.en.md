## Serverless && Information Security Night

📣 Announcing our first meetup of the year, with two fantastic talks from our own
organizers.

🔎 Serverless is an architecture where servers — physical or in the cloud — stop
existing as far as the developer is concerned, and code runs instead in
"execution environments" managed by providers like Amazon, Google, IBM, and
others.

### Talks

1. **Learning serverless with a focus on IoT** — Sergio Florez
2. **Information security in Eje Cafetero institutions, and OWASP** — Santiago Bernal ([profile](https://twitter.com/santiaguf))
