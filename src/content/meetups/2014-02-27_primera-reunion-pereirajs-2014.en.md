## PereiraJs's first gathering

At this meetup [Daniel Aristizabal](https://twitter.com/cronopio2) welcomed the
PereiraJs community and presented the group's goals and objectives.

[Jhonber Jimenez](https://twitter.com/jh0nb3r) and
[Manuel Pineda](https://twitter.com/pin3da) then gave a talk titled
**[JavaScript: history and applications](http://jhonber.github.io/slides)**.
