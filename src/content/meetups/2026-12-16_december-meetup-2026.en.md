## December meetup

December is here and the last call of the year is open. We want to close 2026 the way we opened it: with many voices from the community on stage and a conversation afterwards that always runs longer than planned. Send us your talk and come close the year with us.

It is the month for looking back without ceremony. What you built, what you learned the hard way, the project you abandoned and why, the technology that turned out to be less than promised, what you are taking into 2027. If you did something this year you are proud of, or something you learned far more from than you expected, this is the night to tell it.

As a **lightning talk of 3 to 5 minutes** if you want to pass through quickly, or a **full talk of 25 minutes** if the year gave you more to tell. You pick.

If you have been waiting for the right moment to present for the first time, do not let the year end without it. We will help you prepare the talk.

The date is confirmed. We are still deciding whether we meet in a room or online, and we will announce it here as soon as we know.

See you there!

### Call for speakers

We are taking proposals until Friday 11 December, as a lightning talk or in the full format. It is the last one of the year: if you left something untold, this is the night.
