## December meetup

December closes the year and we close it the way we opened it: many voices, and a conversation afterwards that always runs longer than planned.

It is the month for looking back without ceremony. What you built, what you learned the hard way, the project you abandoned and why, the technology that turned out to be less than promised, what you are taking into 2027. Five minutes if you want to pass through quickly, twenty-five if the year gave you more to tell, and plenty of time to keep talking after.

If you did something this year you are proud of, or something you learned far more from than you expected, this is the night to tell it.

The date is confirmed. We are still deciding whether we meet in a room or online, and we will announce it here as soon as we know.

### Call for speakers

We are taking proposals until Friday 11 December, as a lightning talk or in the longer format. It is the last one of the year: if you left something untold, this is the night.
