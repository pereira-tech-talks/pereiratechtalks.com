## December meetup

December closes the year, and we close it the way we opened it: with lightning talks and a long stretch of talking afterwards.

It is the month for taking stock. What you built, what you learned the hard way, the project you abandoned and why, what you are taking into 2027. Five minutes each and an unhurried conversation after.

The date is tentative and the meetup is online. The call for speakers opens on 15 October.

### Call for speakers

From 15 October we take lightning talks for this date. In the meantime, the general call stays open all year.
