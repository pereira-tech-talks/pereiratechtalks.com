## December meetup

December closes the year and we close it the way we opened it: lightning talks, many voices, and a conversation afterwards that always runs longer than planned.

It is the month for looking back without ceremony. What you built, what you learned the hard way, the project you abandoned and why, the technology that turned out to be less than promised, what you are taking into 2027. Five minutes each and plenty of time to keep talking after.

If you did something this year you are proud of, or something you learned far more from than you expected, this is the night to tell it.

The date is tentative and the meetup is online. The call for speakers opens on 15 October.

### Call for speakers

From 15 October we take lightning talks for this date, until Friday 11 December. In the meantime, the general call stays open all year.
