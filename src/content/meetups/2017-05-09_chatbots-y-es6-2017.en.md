## ChatBots in NodeJs && ECMAScript 6 and Webpack

A great meetup introducing us to conversational interfaces and the ECMAScript 6
standard using Webpack:

You will learn to build chatbots in JavaScript using frameworks and libraries such
as [botkit](https://www.botkit.ai/) and [botmaster](http://botmasterai.com/) to
develop bots for Slack, Messenger, Telegram, Discord, and others.
