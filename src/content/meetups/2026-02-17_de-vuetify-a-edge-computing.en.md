## From Vuetify to Edge Computing — 2026 Opening Meetup

On February 17, 2026 we gathered at **Universidad Católica de Pereira** for
**From Vuetify to Edge Computing — 2026 Opening Meetup**. On the programme:
Juan José Cardona, David Ricardo Rivera Arbeláez.

Our opening meetup of 2026 — reflections on the Vuetify ecosystem plus a dive
into edge computing. Talks, snacks, and networking.

### Talks

**A Figma afternoon that changed the Vuetify ecosystem**

**Speaker:** Juan José Cardona

What Vuetify is, the shape of its ecosystem, and why it matters — told by the person who, out of pure curiosity, ended up creating the Official Vuetify UI Kit for Figma and connecting deeply with the project.

**Multi-agent systems to optimize energy consumption on IoT devices at the edge**

**Speaker:** David Ricardo Rivera Arbeláez

A doctoral thesis in its closing stage on the use of AI agents as a strategy to optimize energy consumption on IoT devices operating under edge-computing schemes. The work explores how autonomous, adaptive agents can efficiently manage available resources and reduce energy spend without harming system performance in resource-limited environments.
