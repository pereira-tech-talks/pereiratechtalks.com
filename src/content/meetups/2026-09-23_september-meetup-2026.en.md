## September meetup

September is coming and we want it full of voices. We are giving the whole month over to lightning talks: three to five minutes each, one idea per talk, and an evening with far more people on stage than usual.

Meetups are the muscle of this community.

See you there!

### Call for speakers

We are taking lightning talk proposals until Friday 18 September. If you have never presented in the community, this is the format we suggest starting with, and the month where we will help you most.
