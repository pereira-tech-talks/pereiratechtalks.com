## September meetup

We open the cycle with what works best for a first time: lightning talks. Three to five minutes each, one idea per talk, and the rest of the evening to talk it over.

The short format lowers the bar on purpose. You do not need six months of research or an architecture that survives millions of users: something you learned that will help the person next to you is enough.

The date is confirmed and the meetup is online.

### Call for speakers

We are taking lightning talk proposals until 9 September. If you have never presented in the community, this is the format we suggest starting with, and the month where we will help you most.
