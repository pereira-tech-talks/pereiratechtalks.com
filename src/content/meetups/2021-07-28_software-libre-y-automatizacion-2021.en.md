## Free software and automation

We have a meetup 🥳🥳🤖 🚀

This time we have a great meetup with the best energy from two talented people
hungry to share everything they know. Join us this Wednesday, July 28, for the
talk.

Talk: Free software for a free society.

Speaker: Steven Mejía

Bio: Steven is a systems engineer, an economist, and a master's candidate in
systems engineering. His master's thesis is a blockchain-based voting system,
currently being used as an "electoral thermometer" for Colombia's 2022
presidential elections.
