## Machine learning, automation, games, and more

On September 26, 2024 we gathered at the **Catholic University of Pereira** for
**Machine Learning, Automatizaciones, Videojuegos y Más.**.

**TALK #1**

**Introduction to machine learning with Unity.**

An introduction to Unity's ML-Agents package for machine learning (reinforcement
learning) in interactive 3D scenes.

**Speaker:** Leonardo Quiñones

**Role:** Software Engineer at Unity Technologies. A computer engineer with more
than 10 years building software, specialized in interactive experiences and
games. Currently doing a master's, researching new technologies and how they can
improve quality of life for patients with motor impairments. Passionate about
games and about sharing what he learns, he is always looking to innovate and pass
on knowledge in tech. His goal is to combine his software development experience
with his interest in health and wellbeing, building technology that makes a real
difference in people's lives.

**TALK #2**

**Exploring MLOps, the key to automating and optimizing your models' lifecycle.**

How the cloud — particularly AWS tools like SageMaker, Glue, and Bedrock — can
carry the whole process, from data engineering through to continual learning. We
also cover accessible on-premise alternatives such as Apache Airflow, MLflow, and
Apache Spark, so you can run MLOps on your own infrastructure without depending
on the cloud alone.

**Speaker:** Nicolás Narváez Olaya

**Role:** Machine learning engineer
