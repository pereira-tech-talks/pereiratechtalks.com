## Meet the Cloud Native Computing Foundation

On March 26, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Meet the Cloud Native Computing Foundation**. On the programme: Andrés
Torres, Daniel David Marcano Hernández.

An introduction to the CNCF, its flagship projects (Kubernetes, Prometheus), and
the right moment to move your workloads to Kubernetes.

### Talks

**Introduction to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation**

**Speaker:** Andrés Torres

What is the CNCF, who backs it, and why does it matter? A tour of its flagship projects (Kubernetes, Prometheus and more) and the community programs and benefits it offers to engineers and chapters around the world.

**How to know when to migrate to Kubernetes**

**Speaker:** Daniel David Marcano Hernández

Key Kubernetes concepts, the reasons and signals that tell you it is time to migrate, real-world adoption lessons from companies running on it, and the benefits and challenges of bringing the technology into your stack.
