## Open Cowork II

On Thursday 19 November 2026 we meet at **Toco Madera Coffee Lab**, Álamos venue (Edificio San Angelo, Cra. 28 #12-14), from 6:30 p.m. It is an open day: come when it suits you.

If you work in technology, lead a venture, or want to connect with Pereira's digital ecosystem, this is a room for networking, moving your projects forward, and sharing a table with the community, in partnership with Toco Madera Coffee Lab.

Juan Alejandro Pérez Bermúdez, Jefferson Arley Villamizar Castaño, and Sergio Estrella are hosting.
