## Cutting-edge technologies: Svelte and blockchain uncovered

On April 25, 2024 we gathered at **Sala Magistral 1 (Edificio 13)** for
**Cutting-edge technologies: Svelte and blockchain uncovered.**.

Join us on April 25 at 18:30 to explore the horizons of web development and….

**Talks:**

**Svelte: the elegance of modern web development.**

How Svelte — through its focus on efficiency, elegance, and simplicity — lets you
build web apps with less code and exceptional performance. We look at its
features, its advantages, the vibrant community behind it, and the key resources
for carrying on learning.

**Why you should learn Web3, and where to start**

You may think blockchain is only for illegal activity or a get-rich-quick bet.
There is far more to it. Blockchain is inevitable, and job opportunities in the
field are growing. In this talk you will find out why you should get involved and
how to start preparing for what is coming.
