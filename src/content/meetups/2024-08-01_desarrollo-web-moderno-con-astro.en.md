## Modern web development: building faster, lighter sites with Astro

On August 1, 2024 we gathered at **Camellando H+W** for **Modern web development: building faster, lighter sites with Astro**.

**Talks:**

**Talk 1:**

**Speaker:** Héctor Julián Cardona

**Role:** Test Automation Engineer SSR at Globant

**Building ultra-fast websites with Astro and TailwindCSS**

An introduction to Astro, a tool for building ultra-fast, framework-agnostic
websites. We look at where that speed comes from, its islands approach, and how
it leans on performance best practices. We also cover why TailwindCSS pairs so
well with Astro and how much faster it makes development.
