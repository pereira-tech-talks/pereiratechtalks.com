## Learning with vibe coding

On June 18, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Learning with Vibe Coding**. On the programme: Jesús David Mena Rivera, Jose
Felipe Duarte.

A meetup on vibe coding — pair-programming with AI agents to ship real software
faster without dropping out of flow.

### Talks

**Learning with vibe coding**

**Speaker:** Jesús David Mena Rivera

What if programming felt more like playing music than solving equations? This talk explores how the vibe coding approach turns learning into something more fluid, intuitive, and personal. Ideal for anyone who wants to learn to program by connecting with their creativity, at their own pace.

**Connecting AI to the real world: powering agents with the Model Context Protocol (MCP)**

**Speaker:** Jose Felipe Duarte

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets language models interact with APIs, databases, and external services through a single secure interface. Learn its fundamentals, real use cases, and how to use it to build autonomous, modular, scalable agents capable of making decisions and taking action in both the physical and digital worlds.
