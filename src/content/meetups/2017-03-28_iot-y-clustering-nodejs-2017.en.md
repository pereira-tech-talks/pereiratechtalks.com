## Introduction to IoT && managing a small cluster with nodeJS

At our second meetup of 2017 we had two very interesting talks on IoT and
clustering with NodeJs:

You will learn what the internet of things (IoT) is and why it is considered a
second industrial revolution, by building smart devices from three essential
components: sensors, actuators, and controllers. Finally you will learn to
program your first robot using Arduino and the popular
[Johnny-five](http://johnny-five.io/) framework for programming hardware with
NodeJs.
