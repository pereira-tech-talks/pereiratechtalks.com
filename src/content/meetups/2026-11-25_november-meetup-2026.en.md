## November meetup

November is for what went wrong. A bug that ate a week, a deploy that fell over on a Friday, an architecture decision you are still paying interest on.

Almost everything told in public is a success story, and that makes it hard to learn anything. Five minutes on how you broke it and what you would do differently is worth more than half an hour of ideal architecture.

The date is tentative and the meetup is online. We confirm it once the programme is closed.

### Call for speakers

We are taking lightning talks until 28 October. Bring your favourite mistake: nothing leaves this room badly, we are here for the lesson.
