## Women in Technology

On March 8, 2026 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Mujeres en Tecnología**. On the programme: Melissa Escobar Gutiérrez,
Mayra Valentina Velásquez.

Our meetup for women's month in technology — celebrating the impact of women in
tech with conversations about creativity, AI, and community.
