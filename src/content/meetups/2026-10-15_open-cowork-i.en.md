## Open Cowork I

On Thursday 15 October 2026 we meet at **Toco Madera Coffee Lab**, Circunvalar venue (Cl. 5 #13a-1), from 6:30 p.m. It is an open day: come when it suits you.

If you work in technology, lead a venture, or want to connect with Pereira's digital ecosystem, this is a room for networking, moving your projects forward, and sharing a table with the community, in partnership with Toco Madera Coffee Lab.
