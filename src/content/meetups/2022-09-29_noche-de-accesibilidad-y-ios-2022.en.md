## Accessibility, design, and iOS development night

**How to get started in iOS development**

Yennifer Hurtado Arce.

I started out as a programmer about 3 years ago, beginning with web (HTML, JS,
CSS, React) at a coding bootcamp called Laboratoria in Peru, which works to bring
more women into technology.

Then I got interested in development specialized in iOS for Apple products and
took my first job at a factory, learning several things along the way, and later
made it to Platzi. Right now I am building a venture in technology, still very
much in progress 😁. I would like to reach more communities, especially people
just starting out who would like to do iOS — there is a lot of demand in that
area too.
