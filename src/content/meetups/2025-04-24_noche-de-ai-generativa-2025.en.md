## Generative AI Night: procedural generation and drug discovery

On April 24, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Noche de AI Generativa: generación procedural y descubrimiento de fármacos**.
On the programme: Cristhian Andrés Grajales Pérez, Frederick Johan Castañeda
Pérez.

Two talks on generative AI — procedural generation in games (Minecraft-style
worlds) and LLMs applied to biotech research.
