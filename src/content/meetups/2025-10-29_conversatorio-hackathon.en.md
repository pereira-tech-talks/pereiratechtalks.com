## PerTT panel: what is a hackathon?

On October 29, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Conversatorio PerTT: ¿Qué es una Hackathon?**.

A panel on hackathon culture — what to expect, and how to organize Pereira's
first one.
