## Continuous Testing: test parallelization and observability

On May 21, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Continuous Testing: Parallelization and Observability**. On the
programme: Sebastián Agudelo Morales, Juan de Jesús Fernández Graciano.

A meetup on continuous testing — scaling test automation without scaling the
team, and turning the suite into a real-time observability tool.

### Talks

**How to scale your software tests without scaling your payroll**

**Speaker:** Sebastián Agudelo Morales

How to run tests in parallel across multiple browsers and operating systems, take advantage of containers and Selenium Grid, and grow test coverage efficiently.

**QA on stream: bring your automated test suite to life**

**Speaker:** Juan de Jesús Fernández Graciano

Learn how to monitor tests in real time with Grafana dashboards and detailed SerenityBDD reports. See how observability, monitoring, and shift-left/shift-right testing let you catch and resolve failures before they escalate.
