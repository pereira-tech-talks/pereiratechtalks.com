## Continuous Testing: test parallelization and observability

On May 21, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Continuous Testing: Parallelization and Observability**. On the
programme: Sebastián Agudelo Morales, Juan de Jesús Fernández Graciano.

A meetup on continuous testing — scaling test automation without scaling the
team, and turning the suite into a real-time observability tool.
