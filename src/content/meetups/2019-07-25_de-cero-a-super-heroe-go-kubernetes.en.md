## From Zero to Hero: learning Go, Kubernetes, and serverless on Google Cloud

📣 An exciting July ahead. Two excellent talks on development in Go and Google
Cloud Platform services:

🔎 If you are looking for a concurrent, parallel, simple, fast, compiled language
with room to grow, Go is one to pay attention to. You could see it as an
evolution of C adapted to building modern applications, with simple and intuitive
syntax. This introductory talk covers a little of its history, its evolution, and
everything you can do with it.

### Talks

1. **Introduction to building modern applications in Go** — Sergio Alexander Florez
2. **Kubernetes && serverless on Google Cloud, from zero to hero** — Mauricio Cuenca ([profile](https://twitter.com/macuenka))
