## Rust Night 2025

On July 23, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Rust Night 2025**. On the programme: Héctor Pulido, Óscar Granada.

A meetup on Rust — the fundamentals, why companies are betting on it, and a live
look at how Rust feels in production.

### Talks

**Introduction to Rust: why does it matter?**

**Speaker:** Héctor Pulido

An introductory guide to understanding Rust from scratch, and why it matters for the future of software development.

**Rust and WebAssembly, JavaScript's inseparable companions**

**Speaker:** Óscar Andrés Granada Baquero

A look at what JavaScript is good at, what it is not, how WebAssembly steps in to cover those gaps, and why Rust makes WASM easier to work with without giving up JavaScript's advantages.
