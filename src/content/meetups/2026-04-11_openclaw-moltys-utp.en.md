## OpenClaw Moltys at the UTP

You can spot a Moltys meetup from across the room: it fills up with the red
lobster claws of the OpenClaw mascot, and with people who came to show what they
are actually building. On April 11, 2026 we gathered at the Universidad
Tecnológica de Pereira for a morning of workshop and conversation, supported by
Cursor.

This was not a long deck in front of a silent audience. The Moltys format makes
room for the community to share configurations, experiments, and the odd — and
useful — connections between agents, messaging, and tooling. Ten real projects in
the room teach you more than the best slide.

### What we put on the table

Sergio Flórez framed the morning: where OpenClaw comes from, what people actually
use it for, and how it fits together underneath. The whiteboard ended up with the
drawing that captures most of the system — agent, OpenClaw, a gateway out to
Telegram, WhatsApp, and Slack, and the tooling side (APIs, skills, MCP, shells).
Sebastián Franco was also among those who presented; other people from the room
will keep being documented in this record as we confirm names and talks.

The architecture matters, but the point of the meetup is the room: questions that
only come from someone who has already hit the wall, tricks that never make it
into the documentation, and a back-and-forth between people building agents on
their own machines.

### Thanks

Thanks to the UTP for hosting us, to Cursor for supporting the event, and to
everyone who showed up with projects — and with the claws on. Mornings like this
are why local communities matter.
