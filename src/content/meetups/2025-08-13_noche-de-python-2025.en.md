## Python Night 2025

On August 13, 2025 we gathered at **Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira** for
**Python Night 2025**. On the programme: Germán Grandas, Jorge Martínez
Gómez.

A dive into Python — from data engineering to web frameworks and AI tooling.
Pereira's Python community showed up.
