## Tech & Soft Skills: AI, remote work, and agile learning

On August 29, 2024 we gathered at the **Catholic University of Pereira** for
**Tech & Soft Skills: IA, Trabajo Remoto y Apredizaje Ágil**.

**Talks:**

**Talk 1:**

**Speaker:** Juan Pablo Ortiz Villegas

**Role:** An engineer by profession and an agilist at heart, Juan Pablo has more
than 8 years of experience managing IT products and services and leading
cross-functional teams. His enthusiasm and expertise bring a distinctive
perspective on integrating artificial intelligence into product management.

**Product ownership with artificial intelligence**

In a world that keeps moving faster, product management has to be adaptive,
customer-centered, and innovative. Join us to talk through effective product
management techniques and see how AI becomes an essential tool for the challenges
in this field. A chance to deepen what you know and share your own experience in
a collaborative setting.

**Talk 2:**

**Speaker:** Juan David Rodríguez

**Role:** CEO of Englishcode.ai and Teilurtalent.com, with more than 10 years
working at technology companies in Silicon Valley and New York. Juan David is an
industrial engineer and CFA charter holder, with a specialization in startup
creation and development from Stanford University. After 20 years away, he
returned to Colombia for the chance to help Latin Americans connect with global
opportunities. His mission is to show that you can live better and grow
professionally faster from Colombia, earning in dollars, than in Europe, Canada,
or the United States.

**How to work remotely for US companies from Colombia — and learn English along the way**

In a globalized world, job opportunities have no borders. Find out how you can
work for US companies from Colombia, earn in dollars, and learn English five
times faster than in a traditional school. We cover effective strategies for
landing interviews and take apart the question everyone asks: "how much English
do I actually need?" to work remotely. Concrete strategies, from a company whose
work is connecting Latin American talent with US employers.
