During this meetup, Daniel Aristizabal welcomed the PereiraJS community and presented the goals and objectives of the group.

Jhonber Jimenez and Manuel Pineda gave a talk titled **[JavaScript History and Applications](http://jhonber.github.io/slides)**.

This was the start of what would become Pereira Tech Talks ten years later — a community that began with eight people in a UTP classroom and grew into a multi-program organization hosting monthly meetups, the annual Pereira Tech Day, the Speaker School, and the La Biblioteca del Mañana reading club.
