At Pereira Tech Talks we decided to create a group on [Telegram](https://telegram.org/) to keep a closer conversation with our attendees. You can join the group here:

http://bit.ly/telegram-pertechtalks

In this group you will find information about upcoming meetups and you can ask any question you have.

If you are particularly interested in JavaScript, you can also join the PereiraJS group, which works the same way:

http://bit.ly/telegram-pereirajs
