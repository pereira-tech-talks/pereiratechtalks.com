From Pereira Tech Talks we want to thank every attendee and every speaker for making this such a great community. It has been a great and successful year for all of us, and we closed it out with a flourish — excellent talks and natillada included, as it should be.

![logo-1](/images/blog/posts/happy-new-year-2018/logo-1.jpg)

In 2018 we hope to keep growing and to share much more knowledge on technology trends and innovation — always looking to spark the curiosity and interest of our attendees and to guide them to grow as great professionals in the tech industry.

Finally, we want to invite you to take a closer part in the community. We have opened a public Telegram channel — you can find it as **"Comunidad Pereira Tech Talks"** — and we created [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSflnDSvCfsdjG3WxgD9HSqvPp0jspH6YNwoTzOvlPVieKwMVw/viewform) where you can apply to speak at upcoming meetups.
