## 🚀 A new platform for our meetups

Dear Pereira Tech Talks community,

We are happy to announce an important update to how we manage our events. From now on, we are using **Luma** as our new platform for organizing and registering meetups.

## Why the change?

After evaluating different options, we chose to migrate to Luma to give you:

- **Smoother registration experience** — faster, simpler flow
- **Better event management** — more capable tooling for organizers
- **More effective communication** — optimized notifications and reminders
- **Modern integrations** — better sync with calendars and social networks

## Your new event destination

From now on, you will find every meetup — and can register for it — at:

**[https://luma.com/pertechtalks](https://luma.com/pertechtalks)**

### What this means for you

- **Future events:** all new meetups will be published exclusively on Luma
- **Simplified registration:** one click to confirm attendance
- **Better communication:** you will get important updates directly
- **Improved experience:** modern, easy-to-use interface

## Thanks for your support

This change is part of our ongoing commitment to improve the experience of our community. We hope this new platform makes it easier to take part in our events and strengthens the bonds among everyone in **Pereira Tech Talks**.

See you on Luma — let's keep growing as a tech community.
