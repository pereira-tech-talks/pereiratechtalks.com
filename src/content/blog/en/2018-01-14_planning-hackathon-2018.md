In January we got together — **[Pereira Tech Talks](https://pereiratechtalks.org)** and **[PereiraJS](https://pereira.js.org/)** — to hang out, plan the new year, and (because geeks) code until dawn.

We radically improved the look of our website and brought both sister communities into one place through a shared content platform.

You can find relevant info about Pereira’s tech communities — plus memories from meetups, workshops, builds, project participation, and past events — at [pereiratechtalks.org](https://pereiratechtalks.org).
