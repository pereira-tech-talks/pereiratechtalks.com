## House rules

<div class="slide-grid-2 slide-grid-2--compact">
  <div class="slide-card">
    <span class="slide-card__icon">👂</span>
    <h3>Pay attention</h3>
    <p>The person on stage prepared for this room.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="slide-card">
    <span class="slide-card__icon">✋</span>
    <h3>Do not interrupt</h3>

  </div>
  <div class="slide-card">

  <div class="slide-card">
