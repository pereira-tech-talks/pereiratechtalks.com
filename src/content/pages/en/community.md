## A community of builders, speakers, and learners

Pereira Tech Talks is not a company. We are an open, volunteer-run community headquartered in Pereira, Risaralda, Colombia, with people contributing remotely from across Colombia and Latin America. This page is a map of how the community actually works: who is in it, how the parts fit together, and how someone new can find their place inside it.

---

## How the community is structured

The community runs on three layers:

1. **Programs (verticals)** — the regular cadence of what happens. Monthly Meetups, the Speaker School, La Biblioteca del Mañana, and the AI Channel. Each program has a clear mission and a lead.
2. **Events** — the moments where everything comes together. Monthly meetups, occasional workshops and hackathons, and the annual Pereira Tech Day conference.
3. **People and channels** — the social fabric. Speakers, contributors, sponsors, and the channels we use to coordinate (Luma, GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, Linktree).

## Roles inside the community

People show up in different ways, and any of them is enough to belong. The most common ones:

- **Attendees** — anyone who shows up to learn, share, and connect. The default and the most important role. Free, every event.
- **Speakers** — people who deliver a talk, workshop, or lightning session. First-timers are welcome and mentored through the Speaker School.
- **Mentors** — people who help others prepare a talk, learn a topic, or grow into a role.
- **Vertical leads** — people who steward a specific program (Speaker School, La Biblioteca del Mañana, AI Channel, Monthly Meetups). They define the cadence and direction of the program.
- **Organizers** — people who keep the operational machine running: events, venues, logistics, sponsorships, communication.
- **Founding organizers** — the small group that has carried the community since 2014 and who continue to steward its mission.
- **Sponsor liaisons** — people who connect sponsoring companies with the community.
- **Press leads** — the contact point for media coverage.
- **Conduct team** — the people responsible for upholding the Code of Conduct.
- **Alumni** — people who were active in a previous chapter of the community and remain part of the network.

The full directory lives at [Contributors](/en/contributors).

## How decisions get made

A short version, expanded in [Governance](/en/governance):

- Day-to-day operational decisions are made by the active organizers.
- Program direction (cadence, theme, content) is decided by the vertical lead of that program in conversation with the organizers.
- Annual planning (calendar, Pereira Tech Day, partnerships) is decided by the founding organizers + active organizers + vertical leads, with input from the community.
- Conduct decisions are made by the conduct team, escalated to founding organizers when needed.

We optimize for transparency over hierarchy: anyone in the community can ask why a decision was made.

## How to join

Three paths:

- **Show up to a meetup.** That is the canonical entry point. RSVP via [Luma](https://luma.com/pertechtalks) and come.
- **Propose a talk.** Use the [Call for Speakers](/en/call-for-speakers) page or the contact form. Talks may be in Spanish, English, or mixed.
- **Contribute.** Read [Contributing](/en/contributing) to find a vertical, role, or task that fits how much time and energy you can offer.
