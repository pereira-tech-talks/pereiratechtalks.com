# Allied communities

Pereira Tech Talks grows alongside other city communities that share venues, calendars, and stages.

## Allied communities

- [PereiraJS](https://perjs.org/) — JavaScript and Node.js
- [Python Pereira](https://pypereira.co/) — Python and data
- [JointDev](https://jointdevweb.firebaseapp.com/) — Cross-stack development
- [QA Conf](https://qaconf.co) — Software quality
- [Backbone UTP](https://backboneutp.com) — Entrepreneurship at UTP

## How we collaborate

- Shared stages at Pereira Tech Day
- Cross-promotion on community channels
- Co-organized thematic meetups
- Public calendar at [/en/calendar](/en/calendar)
