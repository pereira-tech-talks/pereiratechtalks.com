## Why it exists

The tech industry reads short articles, threads and release notes. Almost nobody reads whole books about the craft. The Library of Tomorrow is the opposite bet: once a month, an entire book, a 90-minute conversation.

It is not an academic club. It is not reading purely for pleasure. It is **technical reading with a purpose**: every book is chosen because it sheds light on a decision we make as engineers, founders, leaders or citizens in a society that technology is rewriting.

> The bet: deep reading is rare, undervalued, and exactly what makes engineering judgement compound over years instead of staying stuck in the 6-month cycle of the next framework.

---

## How it works

- **One session a month** — third Wednesday, 7:00 PM Pereira time, hybrid format (in person in Pereira + Google Meet for those joining remotely).
- **One book a month** — announced four weeks ahead so there is time to read it at human speed.
- **A reading guide** — the program leads publish a document with questions, context and discussion points one week before the session.
- **Conversation in Spanish, notes in English** — the discussion is in Spanish (the community's primary language); notes and takeaways are published in both English and Spanish to reach the international community.
- **No exam, no homework** — reading the whole book is ideal but not mandatory. Come with as far as you got; you will still contribute.

---

## Topics we cover

- **Applied AI and agents** — from *Superintelligence* to recent books on RAG, evals and the agentic web.
- **Philosophy of technology** — technology is not neutral; understanding why matters.
- **Science fiction that predicted the present** — Le Guin, Stephenson, Liu Cixin, Stross.
- **Technical leadership and team building** — from *The Manager's Path* to books on staff engineering.
- **Climate, energy, society** — technology as a sociotechnical system, not a sandbox.
- **History of software** — because the past repeats itself under new names.

---

## How the book is chosen

The next book is decided at the end of each session, under three rules:

1. It has to connect with the present or future of technology.
2. It has to be **readable**: if nobody in the cohort dares to read it in a month, it is not the candidate.
3. Curation rotates among the club's active members — nobody monopolizes the list.

## How to take part

- **Write to us** through the [contact form](/en/contact/?topic=the-library-of-tomorrow) with the subject **"The Library of Tomorrow"**.
- We send you the current book, the date of the next session and the link to join.
- If you propose a book and we accept it for a session, **you moderate that conversation**. That is the club's only "responsibility".

## FAQs

**Do I have to finish the book?** — No. Come with as far as you got. The conversation is half the learning.

**What language is the discussion in?** — Spanish (the primary language). Takeaways are published in Spanish and English.

**Does it cost anything?** — No. It is completely free. Everyone buys their own books.

**Can I propose a book?** — Yes. If we accept it, you moderate that session.

**Do I need to be technical to take part?** — No. Being curious about how technology shapes the future is enough.

**Is it in person or online?** — Hybrid. There is a physical location in Pereira and a Google Meet link for those outside.
