# Sponsorship

Pereira Tech Talks (PTT) is a **community-funded operation**. We have no equity, no employees, and no products to sell. Sponsors fund operations: venue rental, AV equipment, snacks, swag, recording gear, speaker travel, and the website infrastructure.

In exchange, sponsors get visibility to a hyper-engaged, technically savvy audience in Pereira (and via livestream, across LATAM and beyond), plus the satisfaction of investing in a public good. We treat sponsor relationships as **long-term partnerships**, not single-event placements.

> **Get in touch:** <sponsors@pereiratechtalks.org>.

## Audience reach

Approximate yearly figures (verified annually in our financial transparency report):

- **Monthly meetups:** ~120–180 in-person attendees + 200–400 livestream viewers per session.
- **Pereira Tech Day:** ~600–900 in-person + 2,000+ livestream + 8,000–15,000 YouTube replay views per edition over 12 months.
- **Newsletter:** ~3,000 subscribers across LATAM, with above-industry open rates (~40%).
- **Discord:** ~1,500 active members.
- **YouTube channel:** ~10,000 subscribers, ~50,000 video views/quarter.
- **Web traffic:** ~25,000 monthly unique visitors (target post v3.0.0 launch).

Audience composition: ~70% Colombia (Pereira and major cities), ~20% LATAM (Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Ecuador), ~10% rest of world (US, Spain, Brazil). Roughly half is engineers, the rest spans product, design, data, ops, and management.

## Sponsorship tiers

We run **annual** partnerships (12 months from signing) with the following tiers. Single-event sponsorships are also available for specific Pereira Tech Day editions; details are negotiated separately for those.

### Diamond — Annual

**Investment:** Negotiated, typically the equivalent of 12,000 USD/year (or local equivalent in COP) — limited to **two** Diamond sponsors per year.

**Benefits:**

- Top-tier logo placement on every monthly meetup banner, website footer, livestream lower-third, and recording end card
- Featured 2-paragraph profile on `/sponsors` with a link to your career page
- One annual lightning-talk slot at a Pereira Tech Day edition (community-relevant content; we co-curate the topic)
- One annual sponsored monthly meetup with venue branding (still community-curated content)
- Logo on the Pereira Tech Day stage backdrop, lanyards, and t-shirts (when produced)
- Speaker scholarship for one of your engineers each year through the Speaker School pipeline (we pair you with a mentor)
- Quarterly sync call with the sponsorship lead and the founding organizer
- Newsletter mention in the sponsor-acknowledgement section, every issue
- Right of first refusal to renew the following year before the tier opens publicly

### Gold — Annual

**Investment:** Negotiated, typically the equivalent of 6,000 USD/year — limited to **four** Gold sponsors per year.

**Benefits:**

- Logo placement on website footer, livestream end card, and recording end card
- 1-paragraph profile on `/sponsors` with a link to your career page
- One sponsored monthly meetup per year (community-curated content; venue branding optional)
- Logo on Pereira Tech Day program and signage (smaller than Diamond)
- Speaker scholarship for one of your engineers per year through Speaker School
- Newsletter mention quarterly
- Sponsor channel in Discord (jobs + announcements, max 1 post / week)

### Silver — Annual

**Investment:** Negotiated, typically the equivalent of 3,000 USD/year — open tier (no cap).

**Benefits:**

- Logo on website footer + sponsor wall on `/sponsors`
- Half-paragraph profile on `/sponsors`
- Mention in the recap blog post of every monthly meetup (silver tier line)
- Pereira Tech Day sponsor signage (smaller than Gold)
- Newsletter mention semi-annually

### Bronze — Annual

**Investment:** Negotiated, typically the equivalent of 1,000 USD/year — open tier.

**Benefits:**

- Logo on `/sponsors` sponsor wall
- Mention in the annual financial transparency report

### Community — In-kind

For sponsors that contribute non-monetary value: venue space, food, beverages, AV gear, swag, services. We treat in-kind sponsors as first-class. Tier benefits are negotiated based on the in-kind value (mapped to monetary tiers above).

Examples of in-kind contributions we love:

- **Venue donation** — a recurring meetup space valued at the equivalent of a Gold tier sponsorship
- **Catering** — food and drinks for monthly meetups (Bronze to Silver depending on attendance)
- **AV gear** — cameras, microphones, lighting (Silver to Gold)
- **Recording / editing** — post-production help (Silver)
- **Swag** — t-shirts, stickers, hoodies (mapped per item value)
- **Cloud credits / SaaS licenses** — for the community website infrastructure (Silver to Gold)

## What sponsorship money does **not** fund

- Salaries (PTT has no employees)
- Equity buy-outs (PTT has no equity)
- Paid promotions disguised as community content
- Anything that conflicts with the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) or the [Community Guidelines](./COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md)

## What sponsors do **not** get

- The right to dictate talk topics for community-curated meetups (sponsored meetups still feature community-curated content)
- The right to access attendee personal data (we never share lists)
- Sublicensing rights to our recordings or photographs
- Naming rights to PTT, Pereira Tech Day, or any vertical
- Direct recruiting outside the `#jobs` Discord channel

## Process

1. **Inquiry** — use [`/sponsor-us`](https://pereiratechtalks.org/sponsor-us) or email <sponsors@pereiratechtalks.org> with a one-paragraph intro and the tier you're considering. We schedule a 30-min call within 7 days.
2. **Mutual fit call** — we walk through your goals, our community values, and the calendar of upcoming events. We reserve the right to decline sponsorships that conflict with our values (e.g., heavy crypto-spamming, aggressive recruiting, dishonest marketing).
3. **Proposal** — within 7 days of the call we send a tailored proposal: tier, deliverables, pricing, deliverables timeline, transparent terms.
4. **Contract** — a simple 2-page agreement (Spanish + English). Signed via email.
5. **Onboarding** — 1-week onboarding for logo assets, brand guidelines, contact roster.
6. **Operations** — quarterly check-in calls, monthly recap newsletter for sponsors, annual transparency report.

## Tax and legal

PTT is currently structured as a community organization without a separate legal entity in Colombia (as of v3.0.0 launch). Sponsorship invoices are issued by a designated organizer's small business tax registration (RUT) and filed accordingly under Colombian tax law. We provide:

- A formal invoice (factura electrónica) for Colombian sponsors
- A bilingual receipt (English + Spanish) for international sponsors
- A clear breakdown of how the sponsorship is recognized in the public ledger

International sponsors with specific compliance requirements (FCPA, ABAC, sponsor disclosure rules) are accommodated — please raise these in the mutual fit call.
